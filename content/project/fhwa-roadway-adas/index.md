## Research question

Which roadway design and infrastructure factors influence the performance of ADAS technologies in reducing rural roadway departure risk?

## My role

I led the proposal development and serve as Principal Investigator. The work identifies and evaluates roadway geometric elements and infrastructure conditions affecting roadway-departure prevention technologies, including LDW, LKA, and LCA.

## Research connection

The project brings roadway engineering into the evaluation of [ADAS and automated vehicle safety](/research/emerging-technologies/). Related prior research includes the published [ADAS effectiveness study](/publication/jsr-adas-effectiveness/).
