I am a postdoctoral researcher in the Smart & Safe Transportation Lab at the University of Central Florida, working with Prof. Mohamed Abdel-Aty. My research connects traffic behavior, roadway conditions, and emerging vehicle technologies with crash risk and injury outcomes.

I received my Ph.D. in Traffic and Transportation Engineering from the School of Transportation at Southeast University in 2023. My undergraduate and master's studies were also completed in the same school.
